Research

Conference Proceedings

A Pooled Exceedance Rate Hides Two Failures in Firm Earnings-at-Risk Under Trade Policy Uncertainty

Siwon Lee

ACM ICAIF 2026 proceedings · Forthcoming

Overview

Evaluates Earnings-at-Risk models under trade policy uncertainty, separating tail misspecification from firm heterogeneity using quantile and conformal methods, with calibration by firm size.

Household Portfolio Choice under Trade Policy Uncertainty with a Deep Operator Network

Siwon Lee

ACM ICAIF 2026 proceedings · Forthcoming

Overview

Extends a Merton portfolio-choice model to include trade-policy uncertainty, jump risk, and stochastic equity volatility, and approximates its HJB solution operator with a multiple-input DeepONet.

Working Papers

The Impact of Tariff and Trade Policy Uncertainty Shocks on Household Asset Portfolios

Siwon Lee

Working paper

Overview

Estimates household stock, bond, and cash portfolio responses to trade-policy uncertainty shocks, and models transmission through firm inventories and imperfect inventory information. The paper develops a Merton-style framework for robust portfolio choice under Knightian uncertainty.

Conference: 2026 ZUEL International Workshop on Frontiers in Finance Research

Domestic Firm Financial Vulnerability Consequences of Exchange Rate Depreciation

Siwon Lee

Working paper

Overview

Studies firm financial vulnerability after exchange-rate depreciation, including heterogeneity by trade openness and financial hedging. It uses Deep Equilibrium Networks to solve nonconvex equilibrium conditions and develops a structural model with endogenous exchange rates and heterogeneous firms.

Homogeneity, Systemic Risk, and the Regulation of Automated Trading in India, the US, and the EU

Siwon Lee

Working paper

Overview

Models correlated automated trading, price feedback, and leveraged liquidation in continuous time, and analyzes position limits, margin requirements, and circuit breakers with reopening auctions across India, the US, and the EU.

Lost in Translation or Strategic Choice? LLM Evidence on Multilingual Risk Disclosure Asymmetry

Wonwoo Kim and Siwon Lee

Working paper · View on SSRN

Overview

Compares multilingual risk disclosures of US-listed Korean and Japanese firms using language models, studies cross-language differences in disclosure, and develops tests to detect and mitigate measurement bias in multilingual text analysis.

Work in Progress

Market Impact of Large Shareholder Stock Sales: An Agent-Based Approach

Siwon Lee

Draft

Overview

Uses agent-based simulations of limit-order-book trading to study liquidity and volatility responses to large shareholder sales, including how information asymmetry, risk preferences, and forecast feedback affect price impact.

Labor Market Conditions and Household Financial Vulnerability

Siwon Lee

Research Proposal

Overview

Builds a state-year panel of labor-market indicators and mortgage delinquency to study links between labor-market vulnerability and household financial distress, including lagged specifications and robustness checks.
