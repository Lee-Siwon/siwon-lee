Current projects

Work in Progress

From Tariffs to Portfolios: Trade Policy Uncertainty and Household Asset Allocation

Second Year Paper

Overview

Studies how tariff policy uncertainty relates to household portfolio choices, combining local-projection evidence with a small open economy model of asset allocation and trade shocks.

Domestic Firm Financial Vulnerability Consequences of Exchange Rate Depreciation

Dissertation Proposal

Overview

Examines firm-level financial vulnerability following exchange-rate depreciation, with attention to differences in trade exposure and financial hedging, and develops a model with endogenous exchange rates and heterogeneous firms.

Asset Allocation under Tariff Policy Uncertainty: A Fourier-Embedded DeepONet Approach with SDE

Draft

Overview

Develops a stochastic asset-allocation framework under tariff policy uncertainty and uses a Fourier-embedded DeepONet to approximate policy functions across Markov regimes.

Market Impact of Large Shareholder Stock Sales: An Agent-Based Approach

Draft

Overview

Uses an agent-based limit-order-book setting to study liquidity shocks and price dynamics after large shareholder sales, including the roles of information asymmetry and heterogeneous investor preferences.

Labor Market Conditions and Household Financial Vulnerability

Research Proposal

Overview

Combines regional labor-market indicators with mortgage delinquency data to study the relationship between labor-market conditions and household financial distress.
